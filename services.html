Good to Know

Questions, answered.

Ducted or split system — which is right for me?
It depends on your home and how you use it. Ducted systems heat and cool the whole house evenly from one hidden unit, while split systems are perfect for targeting specific rooms at a lower upfront cost. During your free assessment we'll recommend the option that genuinely fits your layout and budget — or try our sizing calculator for a quick guide.
Are there rebates available on heating and cooling?
Yes. As a VEU Approved Installer we can access Victorian Energy Upgrades incentives on eligible high-efficiency systems, and we handle all the paperwork for you.
How long does an installation take?
Most split systems are installed in a few hours, and a typical ducted system in one to two days. We'll give you an exact timeframe with your quote and always leave your home clean and tidy.
Do you service systems you didn't install?
Absolutely. Our technicians service and repair all major makes and models of heating and cooling equipment, whoever installed it.
Do you offer a warranty?
Every installation is backed by a 7-year workmanship warranty from Elite, on top of the manufacturer's warranty on your equipment. We're fully licensed and insured.
